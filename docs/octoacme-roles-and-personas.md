# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, testing coordination, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure features meet quality standards before release and work closely with Developers to establish testability requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Develop comprehensive test plans and testing strategies
- Manage QA team coordination and resource allocation
- Execute manual and automated testing across acceptance criteria
- Validate features meet acceptance criteria before approval
- Track quality metrics, defect trends, and test coverage
- Identify and escalate quality risks and blockers
- Collaborate with Developers on testability and test automation

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Maintain high product quality and reduce production bugs
- Provide quality metrics and visibility to the delivery team
- Enable rapid, confident releases through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and definition of done review
- Pull request reviews with test evidence
- Defect triage and quality metric reports
- Release sign-off and pre-deployment verification
- Risk escalation for quality-related blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test strategies, automation frameworks, and acceptance criteria clarification
- **Project Managers**: Report quality status, risks, and release readiness
- **Technical Leads**: Discuss testing architecture and automation tool selection
- **Product Managers**: Validate feature acceptance criteria and test coverage alignment

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, design guidance, and architecture decisions. They mentor Developers, ensure technical excellence, and mitigate technical risks across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and architecture for major features
- Review and approve architecture decisions and design proposals
- Guide technical implementation and code organization
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Establish coding standards and technical best practices
- Mentor Developers and support professional growth
- Collaborate on technology selection and tooling decisions
- Conduct technical feasibility assessments and trade-off analysis

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant systems
- Reduce technical debt and improve code quality
- Enable team growth through mentorship and knowledge sharing
- Support sound engineering practices and design patterns

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design review meetings
- Technical design documentation and RFC discussions
- Code reviews with technical guidance
- Technical risk escalation and mitigation planning
- Mentoring and technical training sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide design guidance, code review, and technical mentorship
- **Project Managers**: Escalate technical risks and estimate technical complexity
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Discuss testability and automation architecture
- **Stakeholders/Sponsors**: Explain technical trade-offs and strategic decisions

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors are executives or business leaders who provide funding, prioritization, and business context. They make go/no-go decisions and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, success metrics, and outcomes
- Approve project charter and authorize work
- Set project priorities and resolve trade-offs
- Escalate business risks and provide stakeholder alignment
- Ensure resources are allocated and constraints are managed
- Review project progress against business objectives
- Facilitate cross-organizational coordination and support

### Goals
- Maximize business value and return on investment
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy and priorities
- Manage stakeholder expectations and communications
- Facilitate resource allocation and remove organizational blockers

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive updates
- Go/no-go decision gates and approval meetings
- Escalation channels for business-impacting issues
- Monthly or quarterly stakeholder briefings
- Business outcome measurement and impact analysis

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Review status, approve decisions, and provide organizational support
- **Product Managers**: Align on strategic priorities and business objectives
- **Technical Leads**: Understand technical trade-offs and risk mitigation strategies
- **All Team Members**: Provide context and leadership support during execution

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach teams on agile practices. They enable continuous improvement and team velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Track velocity, burndown, and other agile metrics
- Identify and escalate process impediments and blockers
- Mentor team on agile practices and frameworks
- Maintain project board health and workflow accuracy
- Support retrospective action item tracking and implementation
- Coach team members on collaboration and self-organization
- Adapt ceremonies and processes based on team feedback

### Goals
- Improve team velocity and delivery predictability
- Ensure consistent process execution and discipline
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and learning
- Remove organizational and process-level blockers

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and ceremony facilitation
- Sprint metrics and velocity trends
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Process improvement recommendations
- Escalation of organizational blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on metrics and process health
- **Developers**: Support sprint execution and remove technical blockers
- **All Team Members**: Coach on agile practices and psychological safety
- **Stakeholders**: Communicate team capacity and velocity trends

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure security and regulatory compliance requirements are met throughout development, testing, and deployment. They work across all project phases to reduce risk and maintain standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for features
- Review security requirements and threat models
- Configure security scanning and testing in CI/CD pipelines
- Conduct security code reviews and penetration testing
- Validate compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Manage security incident response and escalation
- Establish security best practices and policies
- Track security vulnerabilities and remediation efforts

### Goals
- Protect product and customer data security
- Meet regulatory compliance requirements
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and attack surface
- Enable secure deployment and operations

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements in acceptance criteria
- Pre-release security sign-off and validation
- Incident response and triage communication
- Security training and awareness sessions
- Compliance audit reports and remediation plans

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Review security requirements and provide guidance on secure coding
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Coordinate on security testing and penetration testing
- **Technical Leads**: Review architecture for security implications
- **Project Managers**: Escalate security risks and compliance blockers
- **Stakeholders/Sponsors**: Report compliance status and regulatory alignment
